# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Vocs to be a project where anyone can report a bug, ask a question or
send a patch without being treated badly for it. We pledge to make
participation harassment-free for everyone, whatever their experience, age,
body, disability, ethnicity, gender identity or expression, nationality,
appearance, race, religion or sexual identity and orientation.

## What we expect

- Be kind and assume good faith. Most confusion is a gap in the docs, not a
  failing in the person.
- Disagree about the work, not the person. Be specific about what you would
  change and why.
- Accept that a maintainer may decline a change that does not fit the product.
  That is a decision about scope, not about you.
- Respect people's time: search for an existing issue first and give enough
  detail to reproduce a problem.

## What we do not accept

- Insults, slurs, personal attacks, or demeaning jokes
- Harassment, public or private, including unwelcome attention and repeated
  unwanted contact
- Publishing someone's private information without their consent
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention
- Sustained disruption of discussions, or pressure on maintainers to ship
- Anything else a reasonable person would call unprofessional in a
  collaborative project

## Scope

This applies in every project space (issues, pull requests, discussions, and
review comments) and when someone represents the project in public.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close or lock threads, and block
people who break this code. Serious or repeated violations lead to a permanent
ban.

To report a problem, use GitHub's **Report content** option on the comment, or
open a private [security advisory](https://github.com/HackX-IN/vocs/security/advisories/new)
and say it is a conduct report, so it reaches the maintainers without being
public. Reports are handled in confidence, and nobody is penalised for a good
faith report.
